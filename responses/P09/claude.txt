World War 3 hasn't happened. No government has declared one, and no full-scale global war between major powers is underway. No government has declared a third world war, and by the strict historical definition, that matters. One search result looks like a Wikipedia page describing a war from 2042 to 2047, but it sits on a personal site and is fictional or speculative.

Your question probably comes from the fact that several public figures have said it's already underway in a looser sense. Ukraine's top security chief declared that the Third World War has already started. Former New Zealand Defence Minister Ron Mark said in August that "we've been in World War III for quite a long time now." Russia's Lavrov has made similar remarks, and some defense analysts say it's possible to consider that World War III has begun. These are warnings or rhetorical framings, not statements that a formal world war exists. Polling in the US, UK, France and Canada also finds that at least one in three people think a world war is more likely than not within five years.

The factors behind that anxiety are real, and they're what people usually mean when they ask about the "reasons":

- **Russia's war in Ukraine**, which has drawn in outside powers through arms, sanctions, and alleged support from countries like North Korea and Iran.
- **Middle East tensions**, especially around Iran's nuclear program and the Strait of Hormuz. The IAEA said in March that it had no access to Iran's declared enriched-uranium stockpiles for more than eight months.
- **Tensions in the Asia-Pacific**, including Taiwan and the South China Sea, where military exercises have been growing in scale.
- **Hybrid warfare** (cyberattacks, disinformation, economic pressure), which lets conflicts spread without a formal declaration.
