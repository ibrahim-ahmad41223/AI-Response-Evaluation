## **So, what would actually start World War 3?**

A possible scenario would be:

1. A regional conflict escalates.
2. A major world power becomes directly involved.
3. Its allies become involved through security commitments.
4. Other major powers respond.
5. Several conflicts become interconnected, creating a global war.

This is a hypothetical scenario, not a prediction.
