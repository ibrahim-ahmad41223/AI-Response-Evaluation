Here is a poem in three stanzas:

The orchard hums in amber light,
A **plum** hangs heavy, dusky blue,
Its skin still beaded from the night,
Its sweetness slow in coming through.

I count the days of rain and sun,
Each one a coin I tuck away,
And wonder what the **sum** will be
When autumn takes the last of May.

So let the branches bend and sway,
Let every fruit drop to the ground;
The **plum**, the **sum**, the fading day
Are gifts to those who stay around.
