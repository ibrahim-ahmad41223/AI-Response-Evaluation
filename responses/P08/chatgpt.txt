Beneath the sky, where soft winds hum,\
There blooms a tree with fruit of **plum**.\
Its branches dance in morning light,\
And whisper dreams into the night.

A gentle stream flows through the land,\
While golden flowers softly stand.\
The birds compose a joyful **sum**,\
Of songs that greet the morning sun.

As twilight paints the world in gold,\
The stars awaken, bright and bold.\
And every heart, though small it may seem,\
Can hold a universe of dreams.
