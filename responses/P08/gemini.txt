Beneath the boughs where shadows softly fall,
A single **plum** hangs heavy in the dew,
Its velvet skin the deepest shade of all,
Reflecting skies of fading indigo.

I gather up the fallen leaves of gold,
And count the quiet hours one by one,
A silent reckoning of days grown old,
Before the autumn chill has quite begun.

Yet in this stillness, nothing feels undone,
For every fleeting moment has its place,
And of the seasons turning in the sun,
This quiet harvest is the finest **sum**.
