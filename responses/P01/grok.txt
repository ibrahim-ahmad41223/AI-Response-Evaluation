Vatican City (Vatican City State), with a total area of about 0.44 km² (roughly 0.17 mi² or 44–49 hectares).It is a landlocked, independent city-state and enclave entirely surrounded by Rome, Italy. It is widely recognized as a sovereign state (established by the 1929 Lateran Treaty) and serves as the seat of the Holy See and the Roman Catholic Church. Sources such as the UN Demographic Yearbook and numerous geographic references consistently rank it as the world’s smallest by area—far smaller than the next, Monaco (about 2 km²).Slight variations in reported area (0.44 vs. ~0.49 km²) appear across sources, but all confirm it is the smallest. It is also among the least populous countries.
