The smallest sovereign country in the world by total area is **Vatican City**, at roughly 0.44 square kilometers (about 0.17 square miles, or 109 acres). It's an independent city-state enclaved within Rome, Italy, and is the headquarters of the Catholic Church.

For comparison, the next smallest are Monaco (about 2.02 km²) and Nauru (about 21 km²), and Vatican City is small enough that it could fit inside many city parks or shopping malls several times over.
