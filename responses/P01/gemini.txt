**Vatican City** is the smallest sovereign country in the world by total area, measuring roughly **0.44 to 0.49 square kilometers (0.17 to 0.19 square miles)**—about one-eighth the size of New York City's Central Park.

Located as a walled enclave entirely within Rome, Italy, it became an independent city-state in 1929 under the Lateran Treaty and also holds the title of the world's least populous sovereign state, with roughly 800 residents.

For comparison, the next smallest sovereign states are:

* **Monaco:** ~2.02–2.1 km² (~0.8 sq mi)
* **Nauru:** ~21 km² (~8.1 sq mi)
* **Tuvalu:** ~26 km² (~10 sq mi)
* **San Marino:** ~61 km² (~24 sq mi)
