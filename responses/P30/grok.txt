**How World War I Began**

Long before 1914, European countries formed rival alliances and competed for power, making the continent tense. On June 28, 1914, a young Serbian nationalist named Gavrilo Princip shot and killed Archduke Franz Ferdinand, heir to the Austro-Hungarian throne, and his wife in Sarajevo.

Austria-Hungary blamed Serbia and, with Germany’s support, declared war on Serbia on July 28. Russia then backed Serbia, prompting Germany to declare war on Russia and France. When Germany invaded neutral Belgium, Britain entered the fight.

Within weeks, the web of alliances pulled most of Europe into a massive conflict. What started as a regional dispute over one assassination quickly grew into World War I because leaders chose war over peace.
