# **How World War I Began**

Before 1914, European countries were experiencing growing tensions because of competition for land, power, and resources. Many countries were building stronger armies and forming alliances, which were agreements to support one another during conflicts. These rivalries made Europe increasingly unstable.&#x20;

The immediate trigger happened on June 28, 1914, when Archduke Franz Ferdinand, heir to the throne of Austria-Hungary, and his wife Sophie were assassinated in Sarajevo by a young nationalist named Gavrilo Princip. Austria-Hungary blamed Serbia and declared war on it on July 28, 1914.&#x20;

Because of existing alliances, other countries joined the conflict. Germany supported Austria-Hungary, while Russia supported Serbia. Germany invaded Belgium to attack France, leading Britain to declare war on Germany on August 4, 1914. This chain of events turned a regional conflict into World War I, which lasted until 1918.&#x20;
