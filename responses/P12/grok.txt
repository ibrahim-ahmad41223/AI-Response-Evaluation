The student has the core definitions of RAM and ROM **completely reversed**.

- **RAM (Random Access Memory)** is *volatile/temporary* memory. It loses its contents when power is removed. It is used for active programs, data the CPU is currently working with, and temporary storage while the computer is running.
- **ROM (Read-Only Memory)** is *non-volatile/permanent* memory. It retains its data even when the computer is turned off. It typically stores firmware (such as the BIOS/UEFI) that is needed at startup and is not meant to be frequently rewritten.
