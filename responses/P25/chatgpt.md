# **Average monthly salaries of IT professionals in Pakistan (2025)**

**Currency:** Pakistani Rupees (PKR)\
**Period:** January–December 2025\
**Measurement:** Gross monthly salary, unless otherwise specified.

There is no single universally accepted average salary for all IT professionals in Pakistan. Salaries vary considerably according to job role, professional experience, technical specialization, employer, and city.

The most useful approach is to combine industry salary surveys, government compensation benchmarks, and salary records from employment platforms.

## **1. Most reliable sources**

Three particularly useful sources are:

1\. **Pakistan Software Houses Association (P\@SHA):** Its 2024–25 Salary Survey covers 174 companies, 10,126 employees, 92 job roles, and 470 job levels. This is particularly valuable because it focuses on Pakistan's IT and IT-enabled services industry. However, the detailed report requires paid registration.&#x20;

## **6. Limitations of the research**

Several limitations affect the accuracy of any estimate:

1\. **Limited historical data:** Many salary websites continuously update their figures, making it difficult to reconstruct an exact historical average for 2025.

2\. **Different methodologies:** Some sources use employee-reported salaries, others use job advertisements, and some use employer compensation surveys.

3\. **Experience differences:** A fresh graduate and a senior software engineer cannot reasonably be compared using one average.

4\. **Geographical differences:** Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad can have different salary levels from smaller cities.

5\. **International employment:** Pakistani professionals working remotely for foreign companies may earn considerably more than those employed by local businesses.

6\. **Gross versus net salary:** Some figures represent salaries before taxation, while others represent take-home pay.

7\. **P\@SHA report access:** Although its 2024–25 survey is one of the strongest industry-specific sources, the detailed salary tables are behind a paid registration process.

## **7. Conclusion**

The research establishes that IT compensation in Pakistan varies substantially by occupation, experience and employer.

The strongest historical reference is P\@SHA's 2024–25 industry survey, supported by individual 2025 salary records from OpenPay.

However, **the available publicly accessible evidence does not establish one statistically defensible average monthly salary for all Pakistani IT professionals during calendar year 2025.**

It would therefore be misleading to invent a single national average or claim that the illustrative salary ranges above represent verified national averages.
