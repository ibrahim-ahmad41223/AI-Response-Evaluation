# Average monthly IT salaries in Pakistan, 2025

**Bottom line:** There is no authoritative national figure. Most sources put the typical IT professional at roughly **PKR 100,000-150,000 per month** (about US$350-530). Software engineers at larger firms and multinationals earn far more, often PKR 250,000 or above.

## Headline averages by source

| Source | Figure (monthly, PKR) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Paylab | ~148,837 | Average gross monthly pay across the IT category |
| Multiple aggregator sites | ~100,000 | The average Pakistani tech worker is said to earn around PKR 100,000 per month |
| salaryincreasechart.com (June 2025) | ~120,000-150,000 | Average for a software engineer as of June 2025 |
| Peergroup (April 2025) | ~70,000 | Average software engineer salary of around PKR 70,000 |
| WorldSalaries | ~73,625 | Based on an annual average of 883,500 PKR, before tax |
| Levels.fyi | ~259,506 (median) | Median monthly total compensation, based on 122 reported salaries |

The spread is wide (about 70k to 260k) because the sources measure different populations. Levels.fyi draws on self-reports skewed toward larger companies. The lower figures likely reflect the broader market, including small software houses.

## By role

- **Software engineer:** Paylab lists an average of PKR 236,476. A 2025 guide gives a range of PKR 50,000-300,000.
- **Lead developer:** About PKR 248,982 (Paylab).
- **IT director:** About PKR 227,960 (Paylab).
- **DevOps engineer:** 80% earn between PKR 69,545 and PKR 231,285 gross monthly. Paylab notes fewer than 20 verified respondents.
- **QA engineer:** Payscale reports an average base salary of Rs 726,106 per year, which works out to roughly PKR 60,000 per month (my calculation).
- **IT manager:** Payscale reports an average of Rs 920,000 in 2025, or about PKR 77,000 per month (my calculation). It lists a very wide range.
- **Data scientist and AI/ML:** Evidence is thin and inconsistent. A Paylab user entry shows PKR 95,000 per month for a Bachelor's-level data scientist with 3-5 years of experience in Islamabad. Blog-type sources claim much higher ranges, such as PKR 120,000-500,000 monthly.
- **Specialist and high-end ranges** (from a lower-reliability blog): cybersecurity PKR 200,000-700,000, AI/ML PKR 250,000-800,000.

The two sources disagree noticeably, especially at mid and senior level, so treat the figures as indicative bands.

## By employer type

Levels.fyi shows the top end of the market. Systems Limited had a median monthly total compensation of PKR 392,670. A broader summary gives the same pattern: software houses PKR 50,000-500,000, multinationals PKR 150,000-800,000, and freelancing/remote work PKR 200,000-1,500,000+.

## Limitations

1. **No official statistics.** I found no 2025 government or industry-body figures. The most relevant source, P@SHA's salary survey, surfaced only in a 2021 edition, so I did not use it.
2. **Self-reported, non-random data.** Paylab, Levels.fyi, Payscale and Glassdoor rely on voluntary submissions. Some figures rest on very small samples, such as the DevOps data above.
3. **Many sources are low-quality.** Several role and experience ranges come from SEO-style blogs that cite no methodology and may copy one another. I used them only for indicative ranges.
4. **Inconsistent units and dates.** Several Glassdoor pages I found mix annual and monthly labels, and some are old snapshots. I excluded them rather than risk misreporting. A "$1,200 average monthly salary (2023)" claim from ZipDo is also implausible next to the other data (it would be about PKR 340,000), so I left it out.
5. **Gross vs. total pay.** Some figures are base salary, others total compensation (bonuses, stock). Levels.fyi in particular includes bonuses and stock.
6. **Freelance and remote income** is hard to measure. It can far exceed local salaries but is volatile and often unreported.
7. **Currency and inflation.** PKR figures are nominal. Paylab's examples imply roughly PKR 280 per USD, but 2025 values may have shifted.

For a defensible number, I'd treat PKR 100,000-150,000 as the typical mid-market figure and verify against current job postings on Rozee.pk or through a company-specific salary survey.
