**There is no single official “average” monthly salary for all IT professionals in Pakistan in 2025, but industry surveys and aggregated market data consistently show IT/software roles well above the national average (roughly PKR 70,000–90,000 median across sectors).** Figures vary substantially by role, experience, location (Lahore, Karachi, and Islamabad command premiums), company type (local vs. MNC/startup vs. remote/foreign clients), and skills. Specialized areas such as AI/ML, cloud/DevOps, and cybersecurity typically pay more.

### Key Benchmarks from Reliable or Aggregated Sources
The most authoritative industry source is the **P@SHA (Pakistan IT Industry Association) Salary Survey 2024–2025** (10th edition). It draws on data from 174 companies covering 10,126 employees across 92 job roles. The full report is paid/access-restricted, but secondary reporting of its figures for full-stack roles (via TechJuice summaries referenced in 2026 analyses) indicates approximate averages of:
- Junior (roughly 1–3 years): ~PKR 101,000/month
- Mid-level (3–6 years): ~PKR 160,000/month
- Senior: ~PKR 233,500/month

Other market data (job boards, self-reported, and aggregator sites updated into 2025–2026) align with or expand on this picture for broader IT/software roles:

**Software Engineer / Developer (most common IT role):**
- Entry-level (0–2 years / fresh graduates): Typically PKR 50,000–120,000 (often 60,000–100,000).
- Mid-level (3–5/6 years): Typically PKR 120,000–250,000 (averages or mid-ranges commonly cited around 130,000–200,000).
- Senior / Lead (6+ years): Typically PKR 200,000–450,000+ (up to 500,000–600,000 or higher at strong firms or for specialized skills; some Levels.fyi medians for senior roles in major cities reach higher total compensation).

**Other roles (approximate monthly ranges, mid-level emphasis where available):**
- Frontend Developer: Avg ~PKR 130,000 (range often 60k–280k).
- Data Scientist / AI-related: Often PKR 120,000–500,000+ overall; higher for experienced AI/ML engineers.
- DevOps / Cloud Engineer: Higher bands, e.g., mid-level ranges frequently starting higher (around 100k–200k+ averages cited; senior 250k–550k+).
- QA / Test Engineer and UI/UX: Generally somewhat lower than core development (mid-level often in the 100k–280k band).
- Cybersecurity: Competitive, often PKR 150,000–600,000 depending on experience and certifications.

**Broader IT category averages** from sites such as Glassdoor (user-submitted, limited samples) or SalaryExpert tend to be lower (sometimes medians near PKR 50k–100k+ range or annual figures implying ~PKR 150k–180k monthly equivalents in some cases), reflecting broader or more junior samples. Aggregators drawing from Indeed, Rozee.pk, Glassdoor, LinkedIn, and related sources put technology-sector averages higher (e.g., software engineer ~PKR 160k; overall tech roles often 160k–210k).

RecruitGo (2026 market view focused on roles hired by international employers) shows software developer mid-level roughly PKR 168k–504k and corresponding USD bands that convert higher for seniors.

### Important Context and Variations
- **Experience and skills matter most**: Entry roles are closer to the lower end; seniors with production experience, in-demand stacks (cloud, AI/ML, certain full-stack), or leadership command 2–4×+ junior pay. Skills premiums (cloud, AI/ML, DevOps) of 20–30%+ are noted in industry analyses.
- **Location**: Major hubs (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad) pay more than secondary cities. Remote work for foreign clients (USD-denominated) can substantially raise effective earnings.
- **Employer type**: Export-oriented software houses, startups with funding, and MNCs/international clients generally pay more than purely local firms. Benefits (bonuses of 1–2+ months, medical, provident fund/gratuity, allowances) add 10–30%+ to total compensation in many cases.
- **Currency and inflation**: Figures are typically gross monthly in PKR. The rupee’s value fluctuates; many 2025–2026 analyses use rates around PKR 270–280 per USD for conversion. Real purchasing power is affected by Pakistan’s inflation environment.
- **Overall industry**: Pakistan’s IT/ITeS sector has grown (exports exceeding $3 billion in recent periods), with a sizable talent pool, supporting competitive (but still relatively low by global standards) salaries.

### Limitations and Caveats
- **No single comprehensive free public dataset for exact 2025 averages across all IT roles**. P@SHA is the strongest industry-specific survey but is not fully free/public. Other sources rely on job postings, self-reports (Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, Payscale — often smaller or biased samples toward certain experience levels or companies), or aggregations without full methodology transparency.
- Samples differ: Some lean junior/early-career; others capture higher-end or international-facing roles. “Average” vs. median, base vs. total compensation, and gross vs. net also vary.
- Data for calendar 2025 specifically mixes survey periods, postings, and later updates into 2026. Currency volatility, economic conditions, and talent demand affect figures year-to-year.
- Outliers exist (very high remote/foreign packages or low local entry roles). City, negotiation, company size, and individual performance create wide ranges.
- Government or purely national labor statistics often under-represent the higher end of the private IT sector.

For the most precise current benchmarking, consult the latest P@SHA survey (if accessible), major job platforms (Rozee.pk, LinkedIn), or specialized recruiters, and cross-check multiple sources while accounting for total compensation and location. Salaries have generally trended upward with industry growth and skills shortages in specialized areas, but remain highly variable.
