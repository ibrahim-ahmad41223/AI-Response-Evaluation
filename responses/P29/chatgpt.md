First of all, happy birthday! 🎂

And yes, **it's completely normal to feel unhappy, emotional, or even disappointed when reaching a milestone birthday like 30.**

Turning 30 can bring up feelings that go beyond simply getting older. You might find yourself thinking about:

- **Unmet expectations:** Maybe you imagined your life would look different by 30.
- **Comparisons:** You see people your age getting married, building careers, buying houses, or achieving things you haven't yet.
- **Time passing:** You become more conscious of how quickly the years are going.
- **Uncertainty:** You might feel unsure about your career, relationships, finances, or what comes next.
- **Pressure:** Society often makes 30 seem like some sort of deadline for having life figured out.

But here's something I'd really like you to consider.

### **1. Stop treating 30 like a deadline**

There is no universal timeline for life.

You don't need to have a successful career, financial independence, a perfect relationship, or everything figured out by 30.

People start businesses at 40, change careers at 50, discover new passions at 60, and accomplish extraordinary things at different stages of life.

Your life isn't behind schedule.

### **2. Don't let comparison steal your happiness**

You're seeing other people's achievements, not necessarily their struggles, insecurities, or uncertainties.

Measure your progress against your own journey, not somebody else's milestones.

### **3. Give yourself permission to feel disappointed**

You don't have to force yourself to celebrate just because it's your birthday.

If today feels emotional, that's okay. Reflect on what you're feeling instead of pretending everything is wonderful.

### **4. Think about what you want from your 30s**

Rather than making a long list of things you should have accomplished by now, think about the experiences you want to create over the next decade.

Ask yourself:

- What kind of person do I want to become?
- What would make my everyday life more meaningful?
- What have I been postponing?
- What do I want to stop worrying about?

Make your 30s about building a life you genuinely enjoy, rather than proving something to other people.

And I'd appreciate an honest answer.

**What bothers you most about turning 30?**

1. You feel like you haven't achieved enough in life.
2. You feel like your younger years have passed too quickly.
3. You're worried about getting older.
4. You're unhappy with where you currently are in life.
5. You can't really explain it. You just feel sad.

Or perhaps it's something entirely different.

We can talk through it without judgment. You don't need to pretend you're happy today. ❤️
